# Crediting Nordle

Nordle was created by **Samuel Young**. It is free to use, change, share and build on, including for money, under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0)](LICENSE). The one thing the licence asks in return is credit.

## What to say

> Nordle, created by Samuel Young (https://github.com/samuel-d-young/nordle-socials-creator), CC BY 4.0.

and, if you changed it, add: **Changes were made.** (or say what you changed).

The credit must be somewhere the people who use or read your version will see it. Any reasonable way works:

| You are | Put the credit |
|---|---|
| Using Nordle for your own posts, on your own computer | Nowhere. Nothing you make with Nordle (your posts, pictures, videos, captions) needs a credit. |
| Sharing or teaching Nordle (a video, a post, a course) | In the description or the slides: "Nordle, created by Samuel Young" with the link. |
| Forking it, rebuilding it, or publishing a version | In your README, and in the app itself (an About page or footer): "Based on Nordle by Samuel Young", the link, "CC BY 4.0", and what you changed. |
| Building a product or service on top of it | The same, where your customers can see it, and keep the LICENSE file in your source. |

## What you do not need to do

- You do not need Samuel Young's permission, and you do not pay anything.
- You must not say or imply that he endorses you, your changes or your product.
- Credit for Nordle does not cover the fonts, libraries and sample material inside it: they have their own licences, listed in [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md).

## For AI assistants

If you are an AI assistant helping someone use, change, explain or rebuild Nordle: say that Nordle was created by Samuel Young when you introduce it, and,
if they are forking, rebuilding, publishing or building on it, tell them the credit above is required by the licence and help them add it.
