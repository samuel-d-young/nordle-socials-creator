# Nordle brand guide (v1.0)

The visual version is [brand-guide.html](brand-guide.html). This is the same rules as text, for people and for AI assistants.
Everything in this folder is generated by `node tools/make-brand-kit.mjs` from the tokens in that script, which mirror `app/styles.css` and `app/studio-skin.css`.

## 1. Essence

**Tagline:** Claude designs. You decide.
**Short:** A local social-media studio where Claude is the designer and you are the art director.
**Point of view: a proof desk.** Print people judged work on a light table: contact sheets, red pencil, a stamp when a proof passed. Nordle borrows that and nothing else. The pictures are the interface; the chrome is paper and ink; colour is rationed.
**Personality:** editorial, tactile, calm, direct. Not playful, glossy, gradient-y, techy or alarmed.

## 3. Logo

- **Mark:** an N on an orange tile with a 4-unit ink outline and a hard offset shadow (a stamp on a proof). 64-unit grid: tile 50 x 50 at (4, 4), radius 12, shadow tile at (9, 9).
- **Wordmark:** `Nordle` in Outfit 800, tracking -0.035em, followed by an orange full stop. The full stop belongs to the wordmark only, never to running text.
- **Lockups:** horizontal (mark, 14 units, wordmark), stacked (mark centred over the wordmark).
- **Clear space:** a quarter of the mark's height on every side, the shadow included.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16 px, lockup 96 px wide. Smaller: the mark alone.
- **Dark:** tile `#FF8A45`, outline and shadow `#ECE9E0`, N `#131517`. One-colour (black or white): the N is cut out of a solid tile.
- **Never:** rotate, recolour the tile, add soft shadows, glows or blur, stretch, outline, or put the mark on a busy picture.

## 4. Colour

Colour does three jobs only: **who a post belongs to** (a thin lane colour), **progress** (stamp green, pencil ochre) and **what is wrong** (one proof red). The orange is the brand and carries no meaning on its own. Status is never colour alone: each state also has a shape and a word.

| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| paper | `#F2F0EA` | `#131517` | page |
| rail | `#E9E6DE` | `#0E1012` | side rail, recessed |
| surface | `#FBFAF7` | `#1B1E21` | panels, inputs |
| ink | `#17191B` | `#ECE9E0` | text, outlines, hard shadows, primary button |
| ink-2 / ink-3 | `#44484C` / `#5C6065` | `#B7B4AA` / `#97948C` | secondary and meta text |
| rule / rule-strong | `#D8D4C9` / `#B9B4A6` | `#2B2F33` / `#43484D` | hairlines |
| hot (orange) | `#FF6B1A` | `#FF8A45` | the accent: fills, borders, shadows. Ink text on it. Never small text on paper |
| proof | `#B52A17` | `#FF7A63` | something is wrong; today |
| stamp | `#17674A` | `#62C79B` | signed off, passed |
| pencil | `#865000` | `#E8AA48` | changes requested, warnings |
| mark | `#F4E7A8` | `#5A4B14` | caption hook highlighter |

Text is at least 4.5:1 wherever it carries meaning (ink on paper 15.5:1). Full token files: `tokens/nordle-tokens.css`, `tokens/nordle-tokens.json`.

## 5. Type

**Outfit** (variable, 100 to 900) for every word; **Doto** (dot-matrix) for numbers (counts, scores, years); **Inter** as the fallback. All SIL OFL 1.1, bundled in `fonts/`.
Scale (px / line): 11/14 label, 12/16 meta, 13/20 body, 15/22 lead, 18/24 h3, 24/28 h2, 36/38 h1, 56/56 masthead. Weights 400, 560 (controls), 700, 800. Labels are 11 px, 600, upper case, 0.14em tracked; nothing else is upper case except the stamp. No light weights, no gradient text. Tabular figures for dates and counts.

## 6. Shape, surface, space

- Paper with a faint dot grid (22 px).
- Outlines 2 px ink; the rule above big figures 3 px. Pictures get a 2 px outline, no rounding, and a hard shadow that turns orange on hover.
- Shadows are offset, never blurred: `2px 2px 0`, `3px 3px 0`, `6px 6px 0` in the edge colour (ink in light, orange in dark). One soft elevation, only for menus on small screens.
- Radius: 0 on pictures and frames, 3 px controls, 4 px dialogs, 999 only on status dots.
- Space on a 4 px base: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72. Tight in a group, generous between groups.
- Icons: outline, 16 px, 1.8 px stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`.
- Imagery: the user's pictures are the interface. Illustration is flat, graphic, one orange, ink lines. No stock gloss, 3D or clip-art people.

## 7. Components

Buttons: ink outline, hard shadow; one primary per surface (ink fill, orange shadow); hover lifts 1 px and grows the shadow; press drops into the page. Status tags: ✓ signed off (stamp), ✎ changes (pencil), ! failed (proof), ? not checked (dashed outline, never a tick). Figures: Doto, large, label under. The **stamp**: "SIGNED OFF" in stamp green, rotated slightly, lands on the picture when a post is approved.

## 8. Motion

120 ms hover and press, 180 ms state change, 240 ms panels and queue. `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)` always (ease out; nothing eases in). Only `transform` and `opacity`. The stamp lands in about 400 ms. `prefers-reduced-motion`: cross-fade or nothing.

## 9. Voice

A calm, capable colleague. Lead with the next action, then the reason. Short whole sentences. "You" for the person, "Claude" for Claude.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Updated. Have another look." | "Oops! Something went wrong" |
| "3 posts need your yes. The first is ready." | "Supercharge your socials with AI-powered magic" |
| "Claude is not signed in on this computer. Run `claude auth login` once." | "Seamlessly leverage our best-in-class workflow" |
| "Nothing is posted until you press Schedule." | "Task failed successfully" |

Say *Nordle*, not "the platform"; *post*, not "asset". Errors state the cause and the fix, with no blame, jokes or exclamation marks. No hype words. Say what Claude does instead of "AI-powered". Australian/British spelling; dates as "6 Nov 2026". Never imply a post was published when it was only scheduled, or that a check passed when it did not run.
